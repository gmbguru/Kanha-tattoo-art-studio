Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt to get a tattoo?

It varies by person and location. Most clients describe it as a scratching or burning sensation. Our artists take care to minimize discomfort. First-timers get special attention and guidance throughout the process.

How long does a tattoo take?

Small tattoos can take 30-45 minutes, medium ones 1-2 hours, and large designs 3+ hours. Complex designs may need multiple sessions. We can discuss the timeline during consultation.

Are your needles sterilized?

Yes! We use only single-use disposable needles and sterilize all equipment in an autoclave. Safety and hygiene are our top priorities. We follow international standards.

Can I get a tattoo if I'm allergic?

Tattoo allergies are rare but possible. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, inform us beforehand. We can test the ink on a small patch first. Always discuss medical conditions during consultation.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, complexity, and design. Small tattoos start at ₹2000+, medium at ₹5000+, and large at ₹10000+. Check our pricing section above or contact us for a custom quote.
